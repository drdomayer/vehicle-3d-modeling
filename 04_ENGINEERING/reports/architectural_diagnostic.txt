==============================================================================
RANKED FINDINGS
==============================================================================

#1  THREE OF THE FOUR SIGNATURE VOIDS DO NOT EXIST IN THE SURFACE
      front fender channel  depth   0.0 mm
      side intake mouth     depth   0.0 mm
      door channel          depth  36.0 mm at its deepest, 0.0 at both ends
      rear undercut         depth 110.6 mm   <- the only one that works
    The booleans run and report cutting, but the cutters are sized from the RAW
    section profile while the body is built from the SHAPED profile, which is
    wider by more than the cutter's 60 mm outboard margin at those stations. The
    cutter ends up entirely inside the body and hollows it instead of opening it.
    These voids are the car's identity and the reason the highlight test failed.

#2  A 201 mm STEP IN THE REAR CROWN AT specX 2800
      2350: 980.6   2500: 976.6   2650: 957.2   2800: 755.6   2950: 700.6
    The buttress ends at specX 2640 and the deck spine takes over far below it.
    haunch -> buttress -> deck -> tail is therefore two forms, not one. This is
    the direct answer to "is the rear one continuous form": no.

#3  BELTLINE STEP AT THE A-PILLAR IS 239-250 mm
      specX 440 belt 731, 700 belt 720, against a screen base of 970.
    The screen is planted on the body. Note the 150 mm quoted earlier compared
    the beltline at the REAR of the cabin against the screen at the FRONT.

#4  THE SHOULDER TRAJECTORY IS NOT CONTROLLING THE SILHOUETTE
      designed Z 565 at the front axle, body top 772; designed 735 at the rear
      axle, body 984.
    SHOULDER_TRAJECTORY only adds a 13 mm crease at those heights, which is
    invisible against a body 200 mm taller. It is a detail field, not the line.

#5  FRONT WAIST AT THE AXLE IS 115 mm DEEP
      specX -250: 887   specX 0: 772   specX 250: 844
    The arch cut passing through the station. It reads as a pinch in 3/4.

#6  THE HOOPS STAND 357 mm PROUD OF THE DECK
    Tall, but defensible on a roadster. Lowest priority of the six.

==============================================================================
THE THREE CORRECTIONS I PROPOSE BEFORE ANY DETAIL WORK
==============================================================================

1. MAKE THE VOIDS REAL. Size the cutters from the built body rather than the raw
   sections, and verify each one afterwards by measuring surface depth, not by
   trusting that the boolean ran. Without this the car has no signature.

2. BRIDGE THE REAR CROWN. Carry the buttress out to meet the deck instead of
   ending at 2640, so haunch, deck and tail are one falling line with no step.

3. LIFT THE BELTLINE through the cabin so cowl, door and deck read as one line.
   My recommendation is a step of about 115 mm at the A-pillar rather than the
   donor's 70-100: our body sits lower than a 986's, so matching the donor number
   would make the cabin read tall against it.

Nothing else should be touched before the detail phase.
