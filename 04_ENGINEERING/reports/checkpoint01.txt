STATEV 001 — CHECKPOINT 01.  Measured on STATEV_001_v019.blend.
The criterion is docs/17 line 30: the PROFILE — length, height, relationship to the wheels, roof,
nose, rear shoulder, shoulder line. Headlights and diffuser are explicitly NOT judged here.

Nothing is frozen by this document. It records what the seven criteria measure and which of them
can be decided today. The decision itself is the owner's.

=== 1. OVERALL LENGTH — PASS ==============================================
  4370.0 mm.  nose at specX -950, tail at specX 3420.
  Donor OEM length 4315, PUBLISHED. Ours is 55 mm longer, all of it at the front: our nose sits at
  -950 against the donor's front overhang of 1007, which is APPROX.
  MEASURED FACT on our geometry. The comparison to the donor rests on one approx value.

=== 2. OVERALL HEIGHT — OPEN ==============================================
  body 876.5 mm.  floor 120, crown 996.5.
  But the highest point of the CAR is not our body. The windshield header sits at Z 1255 and the
  roll hoops at Z 1235, both donor, both APPROX, both above our crown by 238 and 259 mm.
  Donor OEM height 1290, PUBLISHED.
  This cannot be called PASS while the roof criterion below is BLOCKED, because the two are the
  same question asked twice.

=== 3. WHEEL RELATIONSHIP — PASS, with one caveat ==========================
  front  specX 0     Y +-732.5  Z 323.5   tyre OD 647   tyre top Z 647.0
         body half-width around it 888.1, body top 847.7 — 200 mm of body above the tyre
  rear   specX 2415  Y +-764.0  Z 337.5   tyre OD 675   tyre top Z 675.0
         body half-width around it 923.6, body top 996.5 — 321 mm of body above the tyre
  wheelbase 2415 and track 1465 / 1528 are PUBLISHED, from the workshop manual.
  Caveat: tyre OD is APPROX. If the real tyre is 10 mm larger the arch clearance changes, not the

=== 4. ROOF — BLOCKED =====================================================
  Our deck at the hoop plane, specX 1760, is Z 917.3.
  Windshield base Z 970, header Z 1255, hoops Z 1235 — all three APPROX, all three donor.
  docs/14 marks DECK_SPINE explicitly BLOCKED: its heights were read off the reference render and
  they sit inside the volume the soft top folds into. That volume does not exist as data anywhere.
  The scan plan puts it first for this reason — session S2, the roof in four positions, before
  anything else is touched.
  This is not a fault in the geometry. It is an absence of information, and no amount of modelling
  removes it.

=== 7. SHOULDER LINE — OPEN ===============================================
  Side channel, chord depth measured on the built rings:
    specX 505:41  715:57  925:63  1240:71  1450:72  1765:71  2080:61
  One continuous line from behind the front arch to the intake mouth, largest change between
  neighbouring stations 6 mm through the door itself.
  OPEN and not PASS for one reason: docs/14 carries THREE entries marked "ЗА ПРЕГЛЕД" — the front
  fender top at +85 mm over v016, and the side channel no longer starting over the wheel. A
  criterion cannot pass while its own deviation register has unapproved entries against it.

=== VERDICT ===============================================================
  PASS    3   length, wheel relationship, rear shoulder
  OPEN    3   height, nose, shoulder line
  BLOCKED 1   roof

  CHECKPOINT 01 therefore does NOT pass today, and the reason is not the shape.
  Two of the three OPEN items are waiting for a decision from the owner, not for work.
  The BLOCKED item is waiting for a car.

  What would change the verdict:
    - approve or reject the three deviations in docs/14  -> shoulder line and height move
    - accept that the nose profile is judged without its stage-03 details -> nose moves
    - the roof cannot move without scan session S2. It is the only true blocker.

  A defensible alternative, and the owner's call: pass CHECKPOINT 01 for the FRONT, SIDE and REAR
  zones on the profile evidence above, and leave ROOF explicitly out of the checkpoint until the
  car exists. docs/17 does not forbid a partial checkpoint; it does not describe one either, so
  this would be a new rule and should be recorded as one if chosen.
