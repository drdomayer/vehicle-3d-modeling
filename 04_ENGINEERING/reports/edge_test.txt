  14 of 17 transitions have evidence: a measured crease, a registered panel seam, or a real opening.
  A seam is not a substitute for a class. The class is about the SURFACE; the seam is
  about PANELS. On a real car a G0 break is often a 4 mm gap between two tangent
  panels rather than a crease, so a seam is evidence that the light breaks — but it
  never makes a G1 'satisfied', because a gap is a break and not a transition.

  'lost' is design minus mesh: how much of the designed turn the built mesh fails to
  show. A NEGATIVE value is not a bonus -- it is faceting. A gentle line sampled every
  38 mm becomes a few flat panels, and the dihedral between two of them can exceed the
  smooth turn it is approximating. The main side line reads 15.9 on the mesh against
  6.5 in the design for exactly that reason.

  It measures only the lines it is told about, and it says nothing about whether a
  line is in the right PLACE — only how hard it breaks.
