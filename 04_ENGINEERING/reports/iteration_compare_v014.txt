PROBLEM 1 WAS MIS-DIAGNOSED BY ME. THE 208 mm WAS NOT A CLIFF.
==============================================================================
  specX 350  top Z 921.8 at |Y| 0      <- this is the HOOD CROWN, on the centreline
  specX 420  top Z 947.5 at |Y| 3      <- still the hood crown
  specX 550  top Z 710.5 at |Y| 723    <- this is the DOOR TOP, beside the aperture
  specX 800  top Z 735.7 at |Y| 700    <- door top

The cabin cutter removes everything above Z 640 within |Y| < 700 between specX 420
and 1760. So "top Z" before the cowl is the hood crown on the centreline, and after
it is the top of the door beside the opening. The 208 mm was one measured against
the other — two different features, not a discontinuity in a surface.

The number that actually matters is BELTLINE vs WINDSHIELD BASE:
  820 vs 970  =  150 mm step.
On a 986 that step is roughly 70-100 mm, so ours is 50-80 mm too big, but it is a
different and much smaller problem than the one I reported. Fixing it means lifting
the beltline through the door region, which changes the cabin proportion. Not done
without a decision.
