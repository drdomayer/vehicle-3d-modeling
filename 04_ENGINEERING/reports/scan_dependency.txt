No panel is GREEN. Not one exterior panel can become trusted geometry before the car is
scanned. The design intent is complete and regenerable; the interfaces are not knowable.

The single item that unblocks the most: the roof fold envelope in four positions. It
gates REAR_DECK, which gates ENGINE_COVER, REAR_SPOILER and the whole rear group.
