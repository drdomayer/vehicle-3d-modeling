WHAT PART 2 ADDS TO THE THREE FAULTS ALREADY FOUND

4. THE NOSE IS 105 mm NARROWER THAN THE DONOR AT THE FRONT AXLE
   specX     0  ours 745  donor 850  -105
   specX   300  ours 827  donor 854   -28
   Everywhere else we are wider than the 986; at and just behind the front axle
   we are narrower. The front fender does not cover the donor's own fender line
   there. Since the front fender is bolt-on and thrown away this is not a
   collision, but it does mean the front reads pinched next to the wheel — which
   is the "generic, too soft" front in the renders.

5. THE BELTLINE STEP AT THE COWL IS 139 mm
   cockpit beltline Z 831, windshield base Z 970.
   The screen starts 139 mm above the top of the door. On a 986 that step is
   small; here the glass will look planted on top of the body rather than growing
   out of it. This is the same fault as the cowl cliff, measured from the cabin
   side.

6. THE TAIL RISE IS 106 mm, NOT 38
   At a 250 mm interval: specX 3050 top Z 647, specX 3300 top Z 753.
   Part 1 sampled at 3200 and 3420 and read +38. On the regular grid it is +106.
   The tail dips then climbs again, which is worse than a plain blunt end.

7. WE OVERHANG THE DONOR BY 239 mm AT specX 3300
   The donor body has tapered to half-width 424 there; ours is 663. Our tail is
   a far bigger volume than the 986's. Not wrong in itself, but it is the single
   largest departure from the donor's architecture anywhere on the car, and it is
   at exactly the place the rear reads heavy.
