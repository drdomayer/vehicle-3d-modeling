WHAT THE NUMBERS SAY THAT THE SCREENSHOTS DO NOT
==============================================================================

Three problems in the profile that are arithmetic, not taste. None were fixed —
this is a measurement pass.

1. A CLIFF AT THE COWL
   specX  350  top Z 920
   specX  420  top Z 935      <- windshield base, donor, Z 970
   specX  800  top Z 727      <- door centre
   The body falls 208 mm in 380 mm of length, right where the cowl hands over to
   the door. In side view that is a vertical face, not a transition. The scuttle
   is doing all the work and nothing carries the line into the door.
   Cause: HOOD_SPINE ends at 970 and BELT_DIP takes 48 mm out of the door region,
   with nothing bridging between them.

2. THE REAR PEAK IS IN THE WRONG PLACE
   specX 2000  top Z 985      <- highest point of the whole body
   specX 2415  top Z 823      <- REAR AXLE
   specX 2800  top Z 753
   The crown peaks 415 mm AHEAD of the rear axle and is already falling by the
   time it reaches the wheel. A rear haunch reads as muscle when its peak sits
   over the axle. Here the mass is over the seats instead.
   Cause: BUTTRESS runs 1600..2640 with its rise centred early, and it is what
   sets the crown there, not the haunch.

3. THE TAIL TURNS BACK UP
   specX 3200  top Z 705
   specX 3420  top Z 743      <- the very last station is 38 mm HIGHER
   The tail should fall away. It rises at the end, which is why the rear reads
   as blunt however much the width is tapered.
   Cause: DECK_SPINE ends at specX 3200; beyond it the fallback crown takes over
   and lifts.

Also worth noting, not a fault: at the front axle the half-width reads 745 rather
than the 880 either side of it. That is the wheel arch cut passing through the
station, not a waist in the body.
