=== V. TOP 3 REMAINING ARCHITECTURAL PROBLEMS ===============================

1. DOOR CHANNEL DOES NOT READ THROUGH THE MIDDLE OF THE DOOR
   What I see: the channel exists at both ends and vanishes between specX 920 and
     1400 — exactly the length the eye follows in side view.
   Why: unknown after three attempts. The cutter carries 78-104 mm of depth there
     and the surface does not move.
   Evidence: depth 6.0 / 1.4 / 7.3 / 8.8 / 11.1 mm at specX 920..1400.
   What I would change: stop cutting it and build it into the section profile, the
     way the buttress was just fixed. Booleans have failed on this four times.
   Priority: HIGH. It is the car's main side line.

2. BELTLINE AT THE A-PILLAR IS STILL 217 mm BELOW THE SCREEN
   What I see: the screen still sits proud of the door at the front of the cabin.
   Why: BELT_LIFT raises the section top, but the cabin cutter removes |Y| < 700
     above Z 640 from specX 420, so at the A-pillar the surviving body is governed
     by the cut, not by the lift.
   Evidence: 217.1 at specX 440 against 138.9 at 1635 — the lift lands at the back
     of the cabin, not the front.
   What I would change: taper the cabin cut's front edge upward so the aperture
     starts lower and further back, letting the body carry up to the A-pillar.
   Priority: HIGH.

3. NO VOID GROWS AND RELEASES SMOOTHLY
   What I see: the front channel is one deep spot, the rear undercut is a constant
     slot, the intake mouth appears abruptly at specX 2040.
   Why: the void stations are a coarse depth table and the boolean produces a hard
     edge wherever the cutter meets the surface, with no blend.
   Evidence: front channel -14, 36, 128, 52, 29, 23 across six stations.
   What I would change: drive void depth from a continuous field like the character
     fields, not from a lofted cutter with discrete stations.
   Priority: MEDIUM. They exist now, which they did not before; shaping them is the
     next step rather than a blocker.
