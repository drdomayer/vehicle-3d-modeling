=== PRIORITY 1 — THE SIDE CHANNEL ===========================================
Measured as chord depth: how far the surface sits inside the straight line joining its own two
shoulders, 120 mm either side of the centre. Max-minus-min was the wrong measure — it reads zero
whenever the flank behind the channel is falling, which is most of the car.

  specX     400  505  610  715  820  925 1030 1135 1240 1345 1450 1555 1660 1765 1870 1975 2080
  v016       -4  -11  -20  -17   -4   -3    -    -    -  -15  -10    -    -    -    -    -    7
  v017       22   50   80   66   65   80   83   83   85   79   75   75   74   74   70   75   52

  v016 was NEGATIVE almost everywhere: the surface bulged outward where the channel was meant to
  be. There was no channel, at any station, at all.
  v017:  min 22   max 85 at specX 1240   mean 69   largest station-to-station change 6 mm
         through the door itself. It runs in, holds, and releases.

  Two changes, not one. The channel is now part of the section profile, and it is ONE field from
  the trailing edge of the front arch to the intake mouth rather than a front channel and a door
  channel that met. As two fields they left an 18 mm slack water at specX 550 where one was
  releasing and the other had not built.

  It also starts further back than before. The front arch is a 350 mm cylinder on the axle, so at
  specX 330 it has already removed everything below Z 440 — the channel's own lower shoulder. The
  old table asked for depth there and the surface could not show it. The line now emerges from
  behind the arch, which is where a channel behind a wheel actually starts.

=== REMAINING ARCHITECTURAL PROBLEMS ========================================
Not fixed. Listed only.

1. THE FRONT FENDER TOP ROSE 161 mm AND YOU HAVE NOT APPROVED IT.
   specX 350 went from 678 to 839 to carry the beltline into the A-pillar. It is the right line
   architecturally and the hood crown is untouched, but it changes the front volume and it needs
   your eye on it. If you want it smaller, CABIN_X0 and CABIN_RAMP are the two numbers.
   Priority: HIGH, because it is a change you did not ask for.

2. THE SIDE CHANNEL HAS A 15 mm DIP AT specX 715..820.
   80, 66, 65, 80 across four stations, where every other neighbour pair differs by under 6.
   It sits where the channel crosses the front door shut line. The cause is the depth table, not
   the method. Priority: MEDIUM.

3. THE TAIL TOP IS STILL A SOFT PILLOW.
   The rear profile shows no defined deck edge: the crown rolls into the flank with nothing to
   catch light. The crown line itself is correct and continuous — this is about the edge quality
   of the surface, not its architecture. Priority: MEDIUM.

4. THE NOSE IS FACETED BELOW THE MOUTH.
   Visible in the front 3/4. It is the one remaining boolean on the side of the car meeting a
   surface that has too few sections to resolve it. Priority: LOW at blockout.

5. THE REAR UNDERCUT IS NOW THE STRONGEST VOID ON THE CAR, at 288 mm.
   It is no longer a slot, which is what you asked for, but it is deeper than anything else and
   in the rear profile it reads as the body sitting on a separate lower structure. That may be
   exactly the language you want. It is a judgement, not a fault. Priority: LOW, your call.
