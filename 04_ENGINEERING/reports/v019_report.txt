STATEV 001 — v019. One parameter changed from v018: REAR_UNDERCUT_FIELD.xb 3150 -> 2950.
v018 kept as the rollback baseline, byte-identical. Nothing frozen. Nothing committed at the time
this file was written.

=== THE SELECTION ==========================================================
xb = 2950 was chosen from a controlled A/B/C experiment run on the exact v018 baseline, in which
xb was the ONLY parameter that differed between variants. depth 108, x0 2260, xa 2560, x1 3400,
trans 72 and the edge table were identical in every variant and are identical in v019.

  A  xb 3050  REJECTED — the visual change against v018 was not enough to justify a change at all.
  B  xb 2950  SELECTED.
  C  xb 2850  REJECTED — the release became unnecessarily aggressive: the largest station-to-station
              change went to 58 mm against 50 in v018 itself, at specX 2965..3060.

2950 IS A VISUAL AND DESIGN SELECTION, NOT AN ENGINEERING-DERIVED VALUE. It was picked by looking
at rendered views, not calculated from anything. This is stated plainly so that no later session
treats it as a derived figure.

The only engineering anchors in this region are:
  rear wheel arch ends   specX 2780   (365 mm cylinder on the rear axle at 2415)
  diffuser tunnel starts specX 3145   (DIFFUSER_TUNNEL in statev_skeleton.py)
All three candidate values lay between those two anchors. Nothing in the packaging distinguishes
them; only the eye does.

Also recorded, because it was tested and disproved rather than assumed: xa is a DEAD LEVER for this
shape. Moving it from 2560 to 2780 changed the measured undercut by at most 1 mm at every station,
because between those two stations the undercut band sits inside the rear wheel arch aperture,
where the body has already been cut away. The earlier claim that the plateau was problematic for
starting inside the arch is withdrawn.

=== HOW v019 WAS PRODUCED ==================================================

against the v018 baseline opened from disk. Not an in-memory substitution, and not a copy of the
experiment.

Verified rather than asserted: 9959 vertices, 8899 faces, volume 3.911282 m3, and a sorted-vertex
SHA-256 of 8a29a19de3ae440f58d5f578341cd95ad54e25eac148f5b3043e1296b20fe265 — identical to the
validated xb=2950 experiment. v019 is regenerable from source, as the project rule requires.
